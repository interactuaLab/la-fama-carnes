## Brand & Style

This design system elevates the heritage of traditional Colombian butcher shops ("famas") into a modern gastronomic boutique. The visual atmosphere balances culinary precision, artisanal cuts, and warm hospitality with an uncluttered, contemporary e-commerce flow.

### Design Principles
- **Gastronomic Precision:** Photography and typography convey deep respect for ingredients, freshness, aging processes, and culinary cuts without rustic disarray or bloodiness.
- **Editorial Warmth:** High-contrast editorial serifs meet clean, humanized sans-serif utility, reminiscent of contemporary food journals and high-end charcuteries.
- **Approachable Distinction:** Premium finishes through restrained deep burgundy tones and sand accents, while preserving an accessible, clear shopping experience for Colombian home cooks and barbecue enthusiasts.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep vinous hues, warm tactile surfaces, and culinary accents that reflect maturity, flavor, and craftsmanship.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3A111D` - Vino Tinto Sobrio):** The core institutional anchor. Used for primary interactive actions, high-impact titles, navigation headers, and branded containers.
- **Secondary (`#8B263E` - Muted Rose / Tinto Vivo):** Expressive interactive highlight. Deployed for active states, link hovers, category pills, and seasonal alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#C59B27` - Gold / Ámbar Artesanal):** Accent color for premium distinctions such as dry-aged badges, certified provenance tags, star ratings, and loyalty rewards.
- **Neutral Deep (`#302923` - Carbón Cálido):** Replaces harsh pure black for body text, fine lines, and semantic metadata.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F3EF` - Warm Ivory):** The structural backdrop that prevents clinical sterility, offering an organic paper-like canvas.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used for elevated cards, item containers, and inputs to create crisp separation from the warm ivory ground.
- **Structural Border (`#D8C8B9` - Arena):** Used for tactile dividers, category tiles, and understated border outlines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear distinction between culinary storytelling and commerce utility.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Imparts heritage, high culinary standards, and butcher shop pride. Used for landing page value propositions, cut names, and artisanal recipes.
- **Interface & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability for weights, portions, pricing per 500g/lb, preparation tips, and checkout steps.
- **Labels & Tags:** Rendered in uppercase or semi-bold variants with subtle tracking for cut origin, temperature storage, and delivery estimates.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by structured horizontal intervals.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4-column grid, compact margins of `1.25rem`, gutters of `1rem`).
  - Tablet: `640px - 1024px` (8-column grid, margins of `2rem`, gutters of `1.25rem`).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, canvas margins of `3rem`, gutters of `1.5rem`).
- **Composition Rhythm:** Generous section spacing (`space-xl` and above) gives breathing room to imagery of cuts and culinary pairings, avoiding the compressed discount-supermarket aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic heavy drop shadows, favoring tactile depth created by layered surfaces, fine Sand borders, and subtle warm ambient glows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#F7F3EF` as the base canvas ground.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Modular Paneling):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces framed with a 1px solid border of `#D8C8B9`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating & Sticky Cart):** Subtle warm-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(58, 17, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Cut Customization Modals):** Backdrop blur of `4px` combined with a deep translucent veil (`rgba(48, 41, 35, 0.45)`) and centered modal elevation: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(58, 17, 29, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, structured tailoring (Level 1 - Soft).

- **Standard Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This provides structural solidity reminiscent of classic butcher paper parcels and cutting boards without sterile sharp corners.
- **Badges and Filters:** Slightly more relaxed curves (`0.375rem`) for intuitive tactile clicking.
- **Media Containers:** Strict geometric rectangles with minimal corner smoothing to honor product photography and butcher cuts.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, Buy Now):** Filled `#3A111D` with white text, `0.25rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover transitions to `#8B263E`.
- **Secondary Action (View Cut Details, Recipe Pairings):** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#3A111D`, text in `#3A111D`. Hover fills with `#F7F3EF`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Text in `#8B263E` with subtle underline on hover.

### Product & Cut Cards
- Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px `#D8C8B9` boundary.
- Visual container incorporates an aspect ratio of 4:3 for cut imagery with clean natural lighting.
- Top corner supports floating badges (e.g., "Maduración 21 Días", "Corte Premium") using `#C59B27` or `#3A111D`.
- Bottom region clearly separates the unit weight selector (e.g., "Porción 500g", "1000g") and total Colombian Peso price (`$COP`).

### Chips & Cut Categorization
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#D8C8B9` outline and `#302923` typography.
- Active state: `#3A111D` background with `#F7F3EF` typography.
- Specialty categories (e.g., "Parrilla & Asado", "Madurados", "Cerdo & Aves", "Gourmet Deli") utilize concise iconographic cues.

### Inputs & Weight Selection Toggles
- Form fields feature an ivory ground (`#F7F3EF`) transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on focus with a crisp `#3A111D` border ring.
- Butchery specification steppers (grammage, cut thickness: "Grueso 2.5cm", "Fino", "Molida especial") rely on segmented button groups bounded by sand borders.

### Delivery & Freshness Trust Signals
- Dedicated micro-components featuring cold-chain logistics badges ("Cadena de Frío Garantizada", "Entrega refrigerada el mismo día").
- Highlighted using warm sand containers with `#C59B27` and `#3A111D` typography.
